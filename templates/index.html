FAQ

Questions people ask.

Can PhishGuard guarantee a link is safe?

No tool can. “Looks safe” means we found no strong warning signs. Brand-new scam sites may not be on any list yet, so never enter passwords or OTPs on a site you reached through a message. Type the official address yourself.

Is my data stored or sold?

No. Submissions are analyzed in memory and not stored or logged. There are no accounts, cookies or ad trackers. See the privacy page for exactly which look-ups happen.

What does the Deep scan do?

It looks up how old the domain is and reads the website’s security certificate. Scam sites are very often only days old. It is slower, so it is optional.

How accurate is it?

On our held-out test it flags about 73% of real phishing at “Be careful” with about 6.5% false alarms, and 98% precision at “Likely a scam”. The limits of that test, and a bug we found and fixed in it, are published too.

Does it work for messages in Hindi or other languages?

Link checks work in any language. The message scanner currently understands English phrases best; more languages are planned.

I already sent money or an OTP. What now?

Call your bank immediately to block the transaction, change your passwords, and report it. In India call 1930 and file at cybercrime.gov.in.
