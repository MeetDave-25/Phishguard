About PhishGuard

What it is

PhishGuard is a free tool that tells you how risky a link, a message or a screenshot looks, and shows you every reason, so you can learn to spot scams yourself. Paste something suspicious on the checker and you get a 0–100 risk score in a few seconds.

Who built it

PhishGuard is built and run by {{ site.owner }} (meetdave.tech), as an independent project to help people avoid online fraud, which is rising fast. It is not a bank, a government service or a security company, and it is not affiliated with any brand it names. Questions or problems? Contact us.

How it decides

Four layers work together (see the animated version in How it works):

  • Rules for lookalike brand domains, scam phrases (OTP requests, fake KYC, “digital arrest”), risky endings and hidden redirects.
  • A trained model that rates a web address against thousands of real phishing sites.
  • Live blocklists: the OpenPhish feed and Cloudflare’s security DNS.
  • Link inspection: the server follows redirects and reads the destination page, without running it, looking for fake login forms.

How accurate it is

We measured it on thousands of real phishing sites and popular safe sites, keeping every domain used for testing out of training. At the “Be careful” level it flagged about 73% of real phishing, with about 6.5% false alarms on popular sites. When it says “Likely a scam”, it was right about 98% of the time, but that level caught fewer scams (about 48%).

That means roughly 1 in 4 phishing links still slips through. Brand-new scam sites, hacked legitimate sites and pages that hide from scanners are the hardest. We also found and fixed a serious bias in our first version, and we say so in the full evaluation report that ships with the project. The measurements describe the past; scammers change tactics weekly.

What it cannot do

  • It cannot guarantee that a link is safe. “Looks safe” only means no strong warning signs were found.
  • It cannot see inside your private messages or apps; it only analyzes what you paste.
  • It does not recover money. If you have already paid or shared a code, call your bank at once and report it (in India: 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in).

Money and independence

PhishGuard is free. It does not show advertising and it does not sell data. If that ever changes, this page and the privacy policy will say so first.

Your data

Nothing you paste is stored. Read exactly what is processed and why in the privacy policy and the cookie policy.
